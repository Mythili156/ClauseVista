NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

StartUp Hub ("Disclosing Party")
AND
Consultant Agency ("Receiving Party")

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" includes all technical and non-technical information provided by the Disclosing Party, including but not limited to product designs, customer lists, and financial data.

2. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not use it for any purpose other than the engagement.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Information that is already public knowledge shall not be considered Confidential Information.

4. TERM
This Agreement is effective for 5 years from the date of disclosure.

5. PENALTY
Any breach of this agreement will result in a penalty of INR 10,00,000 and immediate termination of any business relationship.
