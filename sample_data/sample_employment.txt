EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made on this 5th day of February, 2026, by and between:

TechSolutions Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Bangalore ("Employer")
AND
John Doe, residing at Mumbai ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Employee is hired as a Senior Software Engineer. The Employee agrees to devote full time and attention to the business of the Employer.

2. COMPENSATION
The Employee shall receive a monthly salary of INR 1,50,000.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees not to disclose any trade secrets or proprietary information of the Employer to any third party during or after the term of employment.

4. NON-COMPETE
During the employment and for a period of 2 years after termination, the Employee shall not work for any competitor of the Employer within India.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving 1 week notice. The Employer reserves the right to terminate immediately for cause.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement.
